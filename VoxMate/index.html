Packed with High-Performance Features

Everything you need to dominate Voxiom.io in one lightweight desktop client.

Maximum FPS & Low Latency

Powered by V8 JIT aggressive optimization, parallel shader compilation, ANGLE D3D11 backend, and zero-copy memory rendering for buttery smooth gameplay.

SimpleInfo HUD Overlay

Clean, shadowed text HUD displaying real-time FPS, Ping, Position (XYZ), Block & Chunk Coordinates, Velocity, and Yaw/Pitch without clutter.

Custom Crosshair Suite

Load custom image URLs or local PNG crosshairs. Fine-tune width, height, opacity, and pixelated/crisp rendering modes effortlessly.

Resource Swapper & Custom CSS

Swap block textures, weapon audio, and custom skyboxes directly from your local swap folder. Inject custom CSS themes on the fly.

Built-in Ad & Tracker Blocker

In-memory adblocker intercepts third-party ad networks and trackers, preventing random network spikes and keeping your interface clean.

Discord Rich Presence

Showcase your live game status, active server URL, and playing state directly on your Discord profile to invite friends with one click.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Is VoxMate free to use?

Yes! VoxMate is 100% free and open-source software distributed under the GNU General Public License v3.0.

How do I use the Resource Swapper?

Simply place your custom textures or audio files in the VoxMate swap folder, enable "Resource Swapper" in the settings, and restart the client.

Is VoxMate safe to use with Voxiom.io?

VoxMate is an unofficial client designed to enhance rendering performance, UI, and quality-of-life tools. Always adhere to Voxiom.io's Terms of Service.
