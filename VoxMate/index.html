SimpleInfo HUD Overlay

Clean HUD displaying real-time FPS, Ping, Position (XYZ), Block & Chunk Coordinates, Velocity, and Yaw/Pitch.

Got Questions?

Is VoxMate free to use?

Yes! VoxMate is 100% free and open-source software distributed under the GNU General Public License v3.0.

How do I use the Resource Swapper?

Simply place your custom textures or audio files in the VoxMate swap folder, enable "Resource Swapper" in the settings, and restart the client.

Is VoxMate safe to use with Voxiom.io?

VoxMate is an unofficial client designed to enhance rendering performance, UI, and quality-of-life tools. Always adhere to Voxiom.io's Terms of Service.
